02 The full read

Curriculum, strong points, and what each one means for Good Harbor's rollout.

Best pick UC Berkeley Haas

Agentic AI: Strategy, Applications and Organizational Impact

UC Berkeley Executive Education, Haas School of Business
$3,550 Live online · 5 faculty sessions 5 weeks · 4-5 hr/wk Next: Sep 24 - Nov 5 executive.berkeley.edu
Themes · 5 live sessions
  1. How agentic AI systems operate, limits and challenges
  2. Identifying and scaling high-impact use cases
  3. Preparing the org: structure, culture, talent, operating model
  4. Measuring outcomes and articulating ROI
  5. Governance, security, compliance and oversight
Strong points
  • Agentic AI leadership at a top-tier brand
  • Live Haas faculty sessions plus facilitator office hours
  • Capstone: an agentic AI strategy for a real business challenge
  • No published attend-every-session-or-fail rule
Why it matters

Agentic AI is exactly the stack already running inside the finance team: agents for reconciliation, invoices, and reporting. Of the two agentic programs on the page, this is the one whose schedule can survive a travel calendar, and it comes with live faculty access instead of recorded lectures.

The catch: the session calendar and recording policy are not published, so confirm both before enrolling; the content assumes no build experience, so parts will be review; and the brand lands a notch below MIT in a CRE boardroom.

Top AI brand Carnegie Mellon

Leading Enterprise Agentic AI Development

CMU Heinz College Executive Education · Anand Rao
$4,250 Live virtual · 5 half-day sessions Sep 22 - Oct 20 · apply by Sep 15 $1,000 credit toward CMU's CDAIO cert heinz.cmu.edu
Modules · 5 + applied lab
  1. Agent-based systems, multi-agent architectures, enterprise use cases
  2. Data ecosystems, governance, interoperability, architectures for agents at scale
  3. Reliability, safety and compliance: validation, red teaming, monitoring, secure deployment
  4. Aligning agentic AI with enterprise strategy, accountability, value realization
  5. Applied lab: hands-on prototyping with frameworks and case studies
Strong points
  • The strongest AI-specific school brand in the world
  • Built from CMU's Chief Data and AI Officer program
  • Red teaming, validation and monitoring content nobody else teaches
  • Opens a discounted path to the full CDAIO credential
Why it matters

This is the one program pitched exactly one rung above where the finance team already stands: not what agents are, but how to govern, secure, and scale the agent systems already running. Five afternoons, a real lab, and a name that means AI to anyone who knows the field.

The catch: the five sessions run 1-5 pm EDT mid-workday and missing any one of them forfeits the certificate, with no recording or makeup policy published. Fall 2026 is the first cohort and no future dates are announced, so if those five dates do not fit, this one waits.

Best value eCornell

AI Strategy Certificate

eCornell, Cornell Tech · Karan Girotra
$3,750 list · ~$2,625 when a 30% code is live Online, cohort deadlines ~8 weeks · 6-8 hr/wk Next start Aug 5, then every 2 weeks ecornell.cornell.edu
Curriculum · 4 courses
  1. Catalyzing AI transformation: from capabilities to vision
  2. Rewiring jobs, workflows and organizations with AI
  3. Driving AI-powered growth with new products and business models
  4. Designing, de-risking and delivering an AI transformation portfolio
Strong points
  • Purpose-built execution arc, least review for a builder
  • Cheapest of the four when a 30% code is live
  • Ends with an actionable, de-risked portfolio
  • Two years of AI Symposium access included
Why it matters

It ends with a de-risked rollout portfolio aimed straight at Good Harbor's finance workflows: reconciliation, lease abstraction, invoice matching, investor reporting. It assumes the participant will execute, so it wastes the least time, and the asynchronous format bends around an unpredictable close calendar.

The catch: the real price hangs on catching a 30% code (the last one lapsed Jul 31), and the weekly cohort deadlines are firmer than pure self-pacing.

Finance angle Kellogg

AI Strategies for Business Transformation

Kellogg Executive Education, Northwestern · Mohanbir Sawhney
$3,300 Online, self-paced 9 weeks · 4-6 hr/wk Gen + agentic AI Next cohort Aug 27 · reg closes Sep 2 kellogg.northwestern.edu
Curriculum · 8 modules
  1. Foundations of AI: prediction to creation to autonomy (AI Canvas)
  2. AI and customer experience management
  3. AI and operations management
  4. AI and business support functions (incl. finance)
  5. AI applications in selected industries (incl. financial services)
  6. Generative AI and creative industries (ethics, IP)
  7. AI and business transformation (governance, maturity model)
  8. AI and society
Strong points
  • Lightest weekly load of the four
  • Two modules touch finance and financial services
  • Top-five management brand, reads instantly to a board
  • AI Canvas and capability maturity frameworks
Why it matters

At 4 to 6 hours a week, it is the only load on this list that survives a month-end close. Modules 4 and 5 land in finance and financial services, and the Kellogg name carries weight with investors and partners.

The catch: for someone who already builds, the early modules survey familiar ground. It is broad literacy, not a step-by-step rollout plan.

Most complete Chicago Booth

AI Transformation and Leadership

Chicago Booth Executive Education (with Great Learning)
$3,200 Online + monthly live 10 weeks · 8-10 hr/wk Claude workflows module Apply by Aug 6 · start TBA gl.chicagobooth.edu
Curriculum · 8 modules
  1. Enterprise AI Value Mapping Canvas
  2. GenAI co-pilot use-case identification
  3. Agentic AI execution
  4. AID prioritization + AI Use Case Design Canvas
  5. Staged roadmapping and ROI measurement
  6. Change management (Jumpstart, Accelerate, Transform)
  7. Persuasive storytelling for executive buy-in
  8. Playbook integration · plus a Claude-based workflows module
Strong points
  • Cornell's execution focus with a bigger brand
  • Explicit agentic AI and a hands-on Claude module
  • Monthly live masterclasses, real peer network
  • Ends with a finished, org-specific playbook
Why it matters

This is the most complete answer to "lead the org." The Claude and agentic content sits right on top of the stack the finance team already builds with. The arc, map the value, prioritize with AID, sequence the roadmap, manage the change, then sell it upward, is exactly how a rollout gets pitched and staged to Good Harbor's partners. The live masterclasses add a peer network Cornell does not have.

The catch: 10 weeks at 8-10 hours a week with scheduled live sessions is the heaviest commitment on the page, and the next start date is still unannounced.

$3,850 · ~$3,273 with code GROW2026 by Aug 11 Online, self-paced + cohort 6 weeks · 4-6 hr/wk MIT Sloan name Next cohort Aug 19 getsmarter.com

  2. Framing and fixing problems with AI
  3. Multiplying AI impact

  5. Managing an AI-driven organization

  • Built end to end around leading and scaling adoption

This is the one program built start to finish around the exact goal, leading and scaling adoption. Modules 4 and 5 are pure rollout muscle: adapting the workforce and running an AI-driven org. And it carries the loudest name on the page.

The catch: Module 1 is partly review for someone who already builds, and at six weeks the playbook is tighter than Cornell's. Delivered through GetSmarter.

Project capstone INSEAD

Transforming Your Business with AI

INSEAD open online programme
~$2,450 (EUR 2,100) Cohort online + learning coach 5 content weeks + project Next: Sep 21 · apply by Sep 7 insead.edu
Structure · 8 weeks all-in
  1. Launch week, then five content weeks online
  2. Frameworks to align AI strategy with business goals
  3. Designing and implementing AI initiatives across operations
  4. Leading AI-era change across the organisation
  5. Action Learning Project with peer review (2 project weeks)

  • Cheapest live-cohort option of the seven
  • Learning coach support through the project
  • Top-tier global brand

The Action Learning Project is the differentiator: the program forces a finished, reviewed transformation plan for the participant's own organization, which is the artifact a rollout actually needs.

The catch: weekly hours are not published, the brand carries less weight with US commercial real estate investors than MIT or Harvard, and applications close Sep 7.

Where it stands

Berkeley leads this fall.

The best pick: agentic AI leadership on a schedule that can survive a travel calendar. Starts Sep 24.

Carnegie Mellon

The sharpest content and the top AI-school brand, but five unmissable fixed dates. If they do not fit, wait for cohort two.

Cornell

A usable, de-risked rollout plan at the lowest price, from the real university.

Kellogg

Lightest load, finance modules, and a brand a board knows. The easiest to fit around a close.

Booth

Cornell's execution focus plus a bigger brand, live sessions, and agentic content in one package.

MIT Sloan

The loudest name, and the only program built start to finish around adoption itself. Short, on-goal, posted price.

Berkeley Haas

The only agentic AI program on the page, live faculty, September start.

INSEAD

A peer-reviewed transformation project at the lowest live-cohort price.

Berkeley is the best pick this fall: agentic AI leadership with live Haas faculty, and no attend-every-session-or-fail rule on the books; confirm the session calendar and recording policy before enrolling. Carnegie Mellon has the sharper content but five unmissable fixed dates and no announced second cohort. MIT Sloan is the runner-up: self-paced, done by the end of September, and its GROW2026 code expires Aug 11. The rest stay on the board as fallbacks.
