02 The full read

Curriculum, strong points, and what each one means for Good Harbor's rollout.

Best value A · eCornell

AI Strategy Certificate

eCornell, Cornell Tech · Karan Girotra
~$2,625 with code (else $3,750) Online, self-paced ~8 weeks · 6-8 hr/wk
Curriculum · 4 courses
  1. Catalyzing AI transformation: from capabilities to vision
  2. Rewiring jobs, workflows and organizations with AI
  3. Driving AI-powered growth with new products and business models
  4. Designing, de-risking and delivering an AI transformation portfolio
Strong points
  • Purpose-built execution arc, least review for a builder
  • Cheapest of the four with the code
  • Ends with an actionable, de-risked portfolio
  • Two years of AI Symposium access included
Why it matters

It ends with a de-risked rollout portfolio aimed straight at Good Harbor's finance workflows: reconciliation, lease abstraction, invoice matching, investor reporting. It assumes the participant will execute, so it wastes the least time, and the self-paced format bends around an unpredictable close calendar.

The catch: fully self-paced, so no live cohort of peers.

Finance angle B · Kellogg

AI Strategies for Business Transformation

Kellogg Executive Education, Northwestern · Mohanbir Sawhney
$3,200 Online, self-paced 9 weeks · 4-6 hr/wk Gen + agentic AI
Curriculum · 8 modules
  1. Foundations of AI: prediction to creation to autonomy (AI Canvas)
  2. AI and customer experience management
  3. AI and operations management
  4. AI and business support functions (incl. finance)
  5. AI applications in selected industries (incl. financial services)
  6. Generative AI and creative industries (ethics, IP)
  7. AI and business transformation (governance, maturity model)
  8. AI and society
Strong points
  • Lightest weekly load of the four
  • Two modules touch finance and financial services
  • Top-five management brand, reads instantly to a board
  • AI Canvas and capability maturity frameworks
Why it matters

At 4 to 6 hours a week, it is the only load on this list that survives a month-end close. Modules 4 and 5 land in finance and financial services, and the Kellogg name carries weight with investors and partners.

The catch: for someone who already builds, the early modules survey familiar ground. It is broad literacy, not a step-by-step rollout plan.

Most complete C · Chicago Booth

AI Transformation and Leadership

Chicago Booth Executive Education (with Great Learning)
$3,200 Online + monthly live 10 weeks · 8 modules Claude workflows module
Curriculum · 8 modules
  1. Enterprise AI Value Mapping Canvas
  2. GenAI co-pilot use-case identification
  3. Agentic AI execution
  4. AID prioritization + AI Use Case Design Canvas
  5. Staged roadmapping and ROI measurement
  6. Change management (Jumpstart, Accelerate, Transform)
  7. Persuasive storytelling for executive buy-in
  8. Playbook integration · plus a Claude-based workflows module
Strong points
  • Cornell's execution focus with a bigger brand
  • Explicit agentic AI and a hands-on Claude module
  • Monthly live masterclasses, real peer network
  • Ends with a finished, org-specific playbook
Why it matters

This is the most complete answer to "lead the org." The Claude and agentic content sits right on top of the stack the finance team already builds with. The arc, map the value, prioritize with AID, sequence the roadmap, manage the change, then sell it upward, is exactly how a rollout gets pitched and staged to Good Harbor's partners. The live masterclasses add a peer network Cornell does not have.

The catch: 10 weeks with scheduled live sessions is more commitment than Kellogg's anytime format.

Sharpest fit D · MIT Sloan

AI Adoption: Driving Business Value and Impact

MIT Sloan (via GetSmarter) · Paul McDonagh-Smith
$3,850 Online, self-paced + cohort 6 weeks · 4-6 hr/wk MIT Sloan name
Curriculum · 6 modules
  1. Understanding AI today
  2. Framing and fixing problems with AI
  3. Multiplying AI impact
  4. Adapting your workforce with AI
  5. Managing an AI-driven organization
  6. Activating your AI advantage
Strong points
  • Built end to end around leading and scaling adoption
  • The loudest brand name on the list
  • Price is posted, unlike the MIT xPRO programs
  • Ends with a custom adoption playbook
Why it matters

This is the one program built start to finish around the exact goal, leading and scaling adoption. Modules 4 and 5 are pure rollout muscle: adapting the workforce and running an AI-driven org. And it carries the loudest name on the page.

The catch: Module 1 is partly review for someone who already builds, and at six weeks the playbook is tighter than Cornell's. Delivered through GetSmarter.

The call

It really comes down to four.

Cornell

The most practical pick. A usable, de-risked rollout plan at the best price, from the real university.

Kellogg

Lightest load, finance modules, and a brand a board knows. The easiest to fit around a close.

Booth

Cornell's execution focus plus a bigger brand, live sessions, and agentic content. The most complete single package.

MIT Sloan

The loudest name, and the only program built start to finish around adoption itself. Short, on-goal, posted price.

Cornell is the most practical pick: a usable, de-risked rollout plan at the best price, from the real university. Kellogg is the lightest schedule with finance modules, Booth the most complete if a live cohort is worth ten weeks, and MIT Sloan the sharpest adoption focus with the biggest name. No wrong pick in the four.
